urban - asset pack for city campaigns
====================================
Street-level grit: neon signs buzzing, smog drifting between towers,
sirens washing the walls red and blue, litter tumbling down empty
avenues, light pollution bleeding into the night sky. All visual
kinds are authored here; AUDIO IS EMPTY - the default pack's base
audio plays underneath until you drop files in:

  ambience/   loops (voice a visual kind with weather_<kind>.<ext>
              or sky_<kind>.<ext>, e.g. weather_smog.ogg,
              sky_cityglow.ogg)
  music/      looping soundtrack
  sfx/        one-shot {{sfx|name}} cues
  stingers/   musical cue-moment one-shots (tool-only)
  img/        {{img|name}} scene art

WHAT SHIPS HERE
  palettes/   concrete (brutalist grey, hazard yellow),
              rooftop (warm brick night)
  fonts/      poster (Impact-class signage), condensed (narrow sans)
  overlays/   streetlight, panic, surveillance, grunge
  weather/    smog, neondrop, litter, sparks
  skies/      cityglow, skyline, underpass
  pulses/     sirenflash, surge, smokepop   (fire as {{name}} macros)
  decoration/ neonsign, graffiti, caution   (inline {{name|words}})
  chrome/     thinking.svg/css (spray-can crown tag: nozzle roams the
              outline, gems pop, paint drips), thinking_text json/css
              (tagger scrawl: crew-and-concrete wording, bare italic
              condensed), caret.css (neon caret),
              dice json/css (pink/cyan neon tubes bouncing on wet
              asphalt with reflections, traffic-light coin: GO/NO)

Everything merges over the default pack as the global base - same-named
files here override it, anything absent stays the default's. Rescanned
live, so new drops work without a restart.
